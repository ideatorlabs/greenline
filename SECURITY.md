# Security policy

## Supported versions

| Version | Supported |
|---------|-----------|
| 0.1.x   | Yes       |
| < 0.1   | No        |

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue. Email **ideatorlabs.ai@gmail.com** with a description, the
affected version, and steps to reproduce. We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days and
to agree a disclosure timeline with you.

Areas of particular interest: webhook signature verification, token storage, and anything that
could send a message the configured safeguards should have stopped.
